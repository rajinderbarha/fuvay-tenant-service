# Product Decisions Required

1. **`compliance.provider_router`'s `generate_export` rate limiting/throttling.** DPDP data-export generation may have real cost/abuse implications; whether a rate limit or approval workflow is needed is a product question for the module's eventual implementation slice, not resolved here.
2. **`package_commerce.tenant_router`'s `tenant_purchase_package` entitlement/idempotency semantics.** Whether a tenant can purchase the same package twice, upgrade/downgrade mid-cycle, etc., are product questions for that module's eventual implementation slice.
3. **Full-application missing-tenant-mutation sweep.** This slice bounded its "missing mounted route" discovery to the 11 modules already flagged in the canonical CSV's 41 unprotected rows (per the mission's own framing: "Exactly 41 tenant-facing mutation routes are currently classified as not fully protected"). A full sweep of the ~960 mounted mutation routes never previously touched by this initiative was judged out of proportion for a single discovery slice — whether/when to commission such a sweep is a product/roadmap decision, not resolved here (see `known-limitations.md`).
4. **Bundling small modules into future slices.** `non-selected-module-queue.csv` suggests several small modules (admin_catalog's 3 sub-routers, media+profile) could be bundled into single future slices for efficiency — whether to do so, and in what grouping, is left to whoever plans the next slices after 2F-18.
