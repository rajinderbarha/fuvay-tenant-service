# Known Limitations

- No live database/server verification was performed (unit tests + static/executed route introspection only), consistent with every prior slice.
- **Full-application missing-tenant-mutation discovery was NOT performed.** The unfiltered runtime tool reports 1186 total mounted mutation routes application-wide; the canonical CSV tracks only 226, accumulated slice-by-slice across this entire initiative's history. This slice confirmed no missing route WITHIN the 11 modules already flagged by the 41-row discovery scope, but did not attempt to classify persona/genuineness for the remaining ~960 untracked routes — that would be its own dedicated, large-scope discovery effort, out of proportion for this slice (see `missing-runtime-route-audit.csv`).
- **Secondary-gap classifications in `remaining-gap-classification.csv` (e.g. `CROSS_TENANT_IDOR`, `SAME_TENANT_CROSS_OBJECT_IDOR`, `PROVIDER_IMPERSONATION`) are evidence-based INFERENCES from the established defect pattern seen in every other `AUTHENTICATED_ONLY`/`PERMISSION_ONLY` module closed by this initiative — they are NOT independently proven by a live test in this discovery slice.** The implementation slice (2F-18) must directly verify (not assume) whether `platform_notifications.provider_router`'s underlying service methods actually lack thread-membership/notification-ownership checks before claiming closure.
- The `mutation-enforcement-matrix.csv` (per-module summary file) was not updated this slice — its own denominator convention (total mounted routes per module) was not directly affected by the 5 row corrections made to the tenant-only CSV, per established precedent (2F-15C).
- Risk scores in `module-risk-scoring.csv` are evidence-based estimates from source-code characteristics (auth mechanism, route count, apparent capability) — not derived from production traffic data, penetration testing, or a formal threat model, which do not exist in this codebase's available tooling.
