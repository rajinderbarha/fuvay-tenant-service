# Business Profile Specification

Sections (fixture-backed, `TenantProfileFixture`): overview (review-state
banner), business info (legal/display name, vertical, categories), 
registration/tax (registration number, tax ID), contact (owner name/email),
addresses, hours (deferred — not in fixture yet), categories, coverage
(service area count, links to Service Areas page), branding/media (links
to Media page), verification (compliance status summary, links to
Compliance page), owners/staff summary (team size, links to Team page),
account settings (links to Settings page).

Avoid repeating summary data across sections: coverage/branding/
verification/team sections show counts + links, not full duplicated lists
— the full lists live on their own dedicated pages (Service Areas, Media,
Compliance, Team).
