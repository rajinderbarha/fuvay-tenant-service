Terms & privacy overview

Clear roles. Clear records.

This page is a public overview. The current versioned legal documents shown during signup and in the apps remain the governing terms.

Service and payment

The provider is responsible for the on-ground service and its technicians. Customers pay the provider directly. Fuvay usage credits are platform entitlements for providers, not a cash wallet and not withdrawable.

Warranty and resolution

Warranty duration and evidence are recorded against the completed job. A provider may inspect the reported issue before proposing rework, refund or another settlement directly with the customer.

Quality and SLA

Applicable SLA breaches may cause provider usage-credit penalties and affect account health, badges or bookability.

Privacy

Booking information is used to provide, secure and document the service. Customers can use in-app privacy controls for supported data requests. Sensitive credentials are never displayed in public administration views.
