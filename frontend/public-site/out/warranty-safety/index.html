Customer protection

Evidence that stays connected after the technician leaves.

Fuvay keeps the service record, provider details, completion evidence, warranty period and complaint history together.

Downloadable warranty record

When an eligible job is completed, the customer can download a warranty certificate containing the provider and service details needed as evidence.

Provider-customer resolution

Complaints, rework, refunds and settlement proposals are handled directly between the customer and provider. The provider may inspect a warranty issue before proposing a resolution.

Critical safety reports

A safety concern is marked critical and gives the provider a one-hour first-response SLA. Fuvay is not an emergency service; contact local emergency services when anyone is in immediate danger.

SLA accountability

If a provider breaches an applicable complaint or service SLA, platform usage-credit penalties and account-health rules can apply.
